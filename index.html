      GUILD TIP · VERIFY BEFORE USING

      The mount-feed technique

      The bulletin “The Mount Feed Hack” by [PHNX] 1891 says mount feed can earn points even without mount shoes. It describes this sequence:

      1. Check reset availability first. The bulletin recommends resetting the mount to its initial values, but a free-reset interval is not confirmed. Verify the reset cost, cooldown, refund behavior, and current scoring before proceeding.
      2. For a feed-only power-up: untick the “use refund items” checkbox to use new feed. The bulletin says this earns points.
      3. When shoes and feed are required: tick “use refund items” back on. The bulletin says the refunded-item power-ups do not earn points.

      Reported pattern: 4 power-ups with new feed (points), then 5 power-ups using refund items (no points). Treat this as the bulletin’s reported sequence, not a guarantee for every mount or future game version.

      Check the score change and item requirements before repeating. Stop and ask Leadership if the current options or scoring differ.

      SANDSTORM SCUFFLE · GUILD EVENT

      Two squads. One Rising Phoenix.

      Scheduled event window: 5 November–7 November 2026. Individual battle times are not confirmed here.

      Find the event on the guild calendar ↗

      What it is · when and who

      Two guilds fight over structures on the Sandstorm map. Hold structures to earn points; the higher team score at the end wins.

      • Every Friday · 40-minute match · up to 30 players per team.
      • Available slots: 09:00, 18:00 and 23:00 server time.
      • The guild fields two teams in two slots. You can play on only one team.

      These general rules come from the supplied player guide, not a live game feed. Available slots are not confirmed PHNX bookings; check the current event screen and Leadership’s assignments. The recorded 5–7 November window includes registration/event dates, not three battle days.

      Signup and squads

      • Open Events > Sandstorm Scuffle and vote for every slot you can actually attend.
      • Signup unlocks when votes reach 10. At least 10 guildmates must vote for the guild to enter.
      • Leadership posts teams and squads in Discord. Confirm whether you belong to Skirmish Squad A or Skirmish Squad B, your time and your caller.
      • Be online 5 minutes early. If you cannot make it, say so early so a backup can take your spot.

      Check the event’s current registration status. This website does not submit votes or signups. Voting is availability, not confirmation of your final assignment.

      How a match runs

      1. Teleport in: your camp sits on our side of the Sandstorm map.
      2. Prep · 3 minutes: no dispatching yet. Check your squad, caller and first target.
      3. Fight: dispatch squads to structures as they unlock in waves.
      4. End: when the timer runs out, the higher score wins.

      Use prep to locate our Vaults, Altar and the central Temple before the first call. If an unlock differs from this guide, follow the current in-game timer.

      Capturing and moving

      • Send a squad to a structure: empty means you take it; defended means you fight for it.
      • A squad stationed inside is a garrison: it defends the structure and keeps it scoring.
      • Rallies let several players hit one structure together. Coordinate with the caller rather than arriving alone.
      • After a capture, a 1-minute ceasefire prevents attacks on that structure.
      • Squads cannot hop between structures: they return to camp before dispatching again. Travel takes minutes, so every move is a commitment.
      • Sandstorm-only march speedups shorten the trip.

      Structures and rough timeline

      Timing warning: the approximate 5-, 8- and 12-minute unlocks below come from the older 60-minute format. Check the current in-game rules for the 40-minute match; do not treat these as a live countdown.

      Solar Vault · start

      Four on the map, two per side in the side lanes. 50 pts/min, no buff.

      Lane squads take our two immediately and keep them garrisoned. Enemy Vaults are a bonus.

      War Altar · ~5 minutes

      Two on the map, one in front of each team’s spawn. 25 pts/min and +10% Palmon ATK, DEF and HP.

      Strike takes ours when it opens. Always hold it: the buff helps every later fight. Take theirs if we can.

      Healing Oasis · ~8 minutes

      Two on the map, one in the middle of each side lane. 25 pts/min and +25% Armigo healing speed.

      Nice to have when free or the team needs healing. Do not spend the match fighting over it while the Temple is open.

      Miracle Temple · ~12 minutes

      One, dead center. 250 pts/min; no buff known.

      The main fight and primary win condition: worth 10× an Altar or Oasis, or 5× a Vault. Stack our strongest squads here and keep it.

      Priority: Miracle Temple → War Altar → our Solar Vaults → Healing Oasis. Follow the caller when the map demands a different move.

      The math: our half (2 Vaults + 1 Altar + 1 Oasis) earns 150/min; everything except the Temple earns 300/min; the Temple alone earns 250/min. Equal halves are 150–150. Add the Temple to one side and it becomes 400–150. Whoever holds the Temple longest usually wins.

      Team score · holding beats trading

      • Structures earn points while held. Points are tallied every 30 seconds; a captured structure starts scoring for its new owner right away.
      • The battle overview shows the team score and allied ranking.
      • When captured, a structure drops unprotected points that enemy squads can pick up. Losing it costs both future income and the dropped points.
      • Do not swap structures back and forth or leave them empty. Arrange a replacement garrison before moving out.
      • Hit empty or thin enemy structures when our own are covered; their capture drops points for us.

      Camp points and personal score

      A number above a camp is the points it carries. Beat that camp to take them. Your own camp can be attacked too; watch it. Enemy camps with points are secondary targets, only after our structures are covered.

      Earn personal points by attacking structures, garrisoning structures (occupation points), and attacking enemy camps.

      Aim for 15,000 personal points, the reported historical mark for all personal rewards even in a loss. Check the event tab’s current reward track; this is not a guaranteed current threshold. Ask your caller for a scoring assignment rather than abandoning a garrison to chase points.

      Only send PvP-ready squads

      One strong squad that lasts 40 minutes beats four that die in five. Sending every squad when only one is built for PvP can leave your weak squads wiped and you watching the rest of the match.

      Only have one PvP squad? Play with that one. That is normal. If unsure which squad is ready, ask Leadership before dispatching.

      Garrisoning is playing. An uncontested structure still scores and earns you occupation points. Do not leave out of boredom: returning to camp and redeploying costs minutes and may lose the structure.

      Healing · what is actually free

      • Injured Armigos heal free when you leave Sandstorm; match injuries do not follow you out.
      • Healing during the match costs speedups. Use Sandstorm-only healing speedups first; you earn these by playing the event.
      • Regular healing speedups are really spent. Save them for a worst case. Check the item type before confirming.
      • A Healing Oasis gives +25% healing speed; coordinate regrouping when the team is hurt.

      Squad wiped? Heal with Sandstorm speedups, then ask your caller where to go. Free healing on exit does not mean every item used in the match is refunded.

      Roles by power

      • Top accounts · Strike: our War Altar first, then the Miracle Temple.
      • Mid accounts · Lanes: take and hold our Solar Vaults, then support.
      • Lower accounts · Garrison: backfill captured structures so stronger players can move up.

      Every role scores. A garrisoned Vault pays the same no matter who is inside. Low power is not a reason to skip the match; confirm your assignment with Leadership.

      The plan · how we win

      1. Fill the roster: more available squads means more pressure and coverage.
      2. Open on our Solar Vaults as soon as prep ends.
      3. Take our War Altar when it unlocks for the stat buff.
      4. Stack the Miracle Temple with our strongest squads and keep it.
      5. Backfill: garrison players replace Strike as it moves up.

      Then hold. Do not trade structures or chase camps while objectives are uncovered. After the Temple opens, hold what we have, contest the Temple and punish empty enemy structures.

      Communication and common mistakes

      One caller per squad. Follow your caller and report where you are and what you see in guild chat, Discord or voice. Unsure what a call means? Ask before committing to a long march.

      Ping enemy whales: if one player can solo a structure, report where their squads are. Avoid feeding that building and take the rest of the map under the caller’s direction.

      Avoid voting then not showing up, sending non-PvP squads, leaving a quiet garrison, fighting over an Oasis while the Temple is open, or burning regular healing speedups early.

      Why play · rewards

      Everyone who participates gets rewards, win or lose. Winning pays more to each player and the whole guild. Check current reward tiers and eligibility in the event tab.

      • Participants: Prestige, Golden Leaves, Evolution Energy choice chests and speedups.
      • Winning players: extra Prestige, Dreamium V, more speedups and resources.
      • Winning guild, all members: extra Palmon eggs, Golden Leaves, Dreamium V, speedups and resources.

      Armigos heal free on exit, but regular speedups spent during the match remain spent.

      Quick checklist

      Before: voted for available slots; confirmed my team, squad, time and caller; PvP squad set; online 5 minutes early.

      During: PvP-ready squads only; follow the caller; stay garrisoned unless told to move; work toward the current personal reward target (historically 15,000).

      Cannot attend? Tell Leadership early. Can I play both teams? No, one team only. Low power? Yes, join: garrisons win matches and you can still earn rewards.

      PHNX · COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

      Let’s build the best community together!

      PHNX guild PMail “Community guidelines”, issued 2 November 2026, 07:27.

      Guild expectations

      • Inclusivity: Zero tolerance for racism, homophobia, ableism, personal attacks, or toxicity.
      • Disagreements: Resolve conflicts via calm dialogue. If that fails, contact Leadership immediately.
      • NAP violations: Report to Leadership. NAP violations are now tracked and repeated offences punished. We need reports to enforce this.
      • Communication: Read all PMails, DMs, and bulletins from Leadership. Attend events you signed up for.

      Activity rules

      • Absences: Report inactivity over 2 days to Leadership with a return date to get “Immunities” status.
      • No notice: Day 2 unannounced absence = demotion to R2. Day 3 = demotion to R1 (risk of being kicked if there is a recruitment queue).

      The accountability process

      If rules are broken, we use a firm 4-step system:

      1. Review: Leadership asks you to re-read this text and confirm you understand it.
      2. Formal warning: A strict, documented warning regarding the behavior.
      3. R1 demotion: Moved to R1. Here you risk immediate kick if we have a player queue.
      4. Removal: Permanent removal from PHNX.

      REALM POLICY · SERVER #268

      Server rules — #268

      Realm PMail “Server rules for all of #268” by [AVAL] Lord.Vader, issued 29 October 2026, 14:22.

      Our goal is to maintain cooperation, prevent internal conflict, and keep #268 united against other servers. These server-wide rules are separate from PHNX’s guild accountability process above.

      1. No attacks on #268 players or guilds

      No attacks on camps, guild buildings, or property for kills (unless guildless), resources, retaliation, or personal disputes unless permitted by event rules, player agreements, or server agreements.

      2. Airships & Dojos

      Do not compete against #268 players in Airships or Dojos.

      3. No retaliation

      Do not retaliate against rule breakers. Screenshot the incident and report it to your R4/R5. Let guild leadership resolve disputes diplomatically.

      4. Evidence & reporting

      Provide screenshots or battle reports, player name, guild, incident time, and a brief explanation whenever possible.

      5. Violations & consequences

      • Minor/first offense: Warning and resolution between guild leaders.
      • Repeated offenses: Escalation between R5s.
      • Serious/deliberate offenses: Removal from the guild. Other guilds should not recruit the player until the issue is resolved. This may also lead to an attack on the camp.

      Accidental mistakes will be considered, but deliberate or repeated violations will not be tolerated.

      6. Leadership responsibility

      R4s and R5s must communicate these rules, enforce them, and promptly address violations.

      Work together, resolve disputes through leadership, and keep #268 united against external competition.

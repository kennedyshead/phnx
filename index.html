    GUILD TIP · VERIFY BEFORE USING

    The mount-feed technique

    The bulletin “The Mount Feed Hack” by [PHNX] 1891 says mount feed can earn points even without mount shoes. It describes this sequence:

    1. Check reset availability first. The bulletin recommends resetting the mount to its initial values, but a free-reset interval is not confirmed. Verify the reset cost, cooldown, refund behavior, and current scoring before proceeding.
    2. For a feed-only power-up: untick the “use refund items” checkbox to use new feed. The bulletin says this earns points.
    3. When shoes and feed are required: tick “use refund items” back on. The bulletin says the refunded-item power-ups do not earn points.

    Reported pattern: 4 power-ups with new feed (points), then 5 power-ups using refund items (no points). Treat this as the bulletin’s reported sequence, not a guarantee for every mount or future game version.

    Check the score change and item requirements before repeating. Stop and ask Leadership if the current options or scoring differ.

    Guild tip: “The Mount Feed Hack” by [PHNX] 1891, 2 November 2026, 12:44. Scoring and reset details have not been independently verified.

    PHNX · COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

    Let’s build the best community together!

    PHNX guild PMail “Community guidelines”, issued 2 November 2026, 07:27.

    Guild expectations

    • Inclusivity: Zero tolerance for racism, homophobia, ableism, personal attacks, or toxicity.
    • Disagreements: Resolve conflicts via calm dialogue. If that fails, contact Leadership immediately.
    • NAP violations: Report to Leadership. NAP violations are now tracked and repeated offences punished. We need reports to enforce this.
    • Communication: Read all PMails, DMs, and bulletins from Leadership. Attend events you signed up for.

    Activity rules

    • Absences: Report inactivity over 2 days to Leadership with a return date to get “Immunities” status.
    • No notice: Day 2 unannounced absence = demotion to R2. Day 3 = demotion to R1 (risk of being kicked if there is a recruitment queue).

    The accountability process

    If rules are broken, we use a firm 4-step system:

    1. Review: Leadership asks you to re-read this text and confirm you understand it.
    2. Formal warning: A strict, documented warning regarding the behavior.
    3. R1 demotion: Moved to R1. Here you risk immediate kick if we have a player queue.
    4. Removal: Permanent removal from PHNX.

    REALM POLICY · SERVER #268

    Server rules — #268

    Realm PMail “Server rules for all of #268” by [AVAL] Lord.Vader, issued 29 October 2026, 14:22.

    Our goal is to maintain cooperation, prevent internal conflict, and keep #268 united against other servers. These server-wide rules are separate from PHNX’s guild accountability process above.

    1. No attacks on #268 players or guilds

    No attacks on camps, guild buildings, or property for kills (unless guildless), resources, retaliation, or personal disputes unless permitted by event rules, player agreements, or server agreements.

    2. Airships & Dojos

    Do not compete against #268 players in Airships or Dojos.

    3. No retaliation

    Do not retaliate against rule breakers. Screenshot the incident and report it to your R4/R5. Let guild leadership resolve disputes diplomatically.

    4. Evidence & reporting

    Provide screenshots or battle reports, player name, guild, incident time, and a brief explanation whenever possible.

    5. Violations & consequences

    • Minor/first offense: Warning and resolution between guild leaders.
    • Repeated offenses: Escalation between R5s.
    • Serious/deliberate offenses: Removal from the guild. Other guilds should not recruit the player until the issue is resolved. This may also lead to an attack on the camp.

    Accidental mistakes will be considered, but deliberate or repeated violations will not be tolerated.

    6. Leadership responsibility

    R4s and R5s must communicate these rules, enforce them, and promptly address violations.

    Work together, resolve disputes through leadership, and keep #268 united against external competition.

      ARCTIC SHOWDOWN · PLAYER GUIDE

      For players

      Know your position and confirm your target before using an attack.

      Event overview ↗

      Attacks and stars

      Rules checked against the supplied in-game Details screens on 3 November 2026.

      • You have 2 attacks daily; VIP 14+ receives one extra attack. The game allows any enemy target, but follow PHNX target assignments.
      • All your squads combine into a rally squad against the enemy defending rally squad.
      • Win: 3 stars. On a loss, eliminate more than 75% of defending Armigo for 2 stars, or more than 50% for 1 star. The thresholds are strictly more than, not at least.
      • Regular squads are applied by default and filled with maximum-level Armigo, regardless of Camp supply. Event Armigo losses do not affect your Camp.

      Who can take part?

      All guildmates present at registration can attack, not just the selected defenders. Defenders also set defence lineups for their separate buildings; Guild Guardians only defend.

      Leaving the guild disqualifies you from remaining matches, even if you rejoin. Members joining during registration need officers to re-register them to participate.

      Before using your attacks

      Suggested preparation checklist, not additional verified event mechanics:

      • Check the current event phase, attack availability, and deadline in-game.
      • Confirm your position number and assigned target before attacking.
      • Read current guild instructions and coordinate to avoid taking someone else’s target.
      • After your first attack, share the result and ask for your next assignment if it is unclear.
      • Check current scoring and tie-break rules in-game rather than assuming an older guide still applies.

      ARCTIC SHOWDOWN · OFFICER PLAYBOOK

      For officers & callers

      Keep target assignments clear and avoid overlapping attacks.

      Event overview ↗

      Registration and defenders

      The Guildmaster or R4 officers register 5–30 defenders. Guild Guardians automatically fill the roster to 30 after registration; they cannot attack. Selected defenders need both attack and defence lineups, while other eligible guildmates focus on attacking.

      Check new arrivals during registration and re-register them as needed. Remind everyone that leaving, even temporarily, disqualifies them from remaining matches.

      Guild scoring and progression

      The guild with more stars wins; equal star totals draw. All guildmates on both sides receive rewards based on the outcome.

      Qualifier wins earn points, losses lose points, and draws award the same points to both sides. The top 16 guilds by total qualifier points advance to head-to-head elimination: Round of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals and finals. Qualifier battle rules also apply in knockouts, and final rankings determine rewards. No knockout draw-resolution rule is supplied here; check in-game rather than inventing a tie-break.

      PHNX numbered-attack guidance

      Confirm the current roster and position numbers before sharing assignments. These guild instructions are undated and may change.

      • First attacks match position numbers: position #1 attacks #1.
      • Reserve weaker side targets for their intended players.
      • Give explicit follow-up assignments when a target is occupied or an attack is complete; do not let players automatically move to the next number.

      This is PHNX Arctic Showdown guidance, not a universal game rule or Ember Wars instruction. Keep target/member details in guild channels, not on this public site.

      What scores each day

      Open a day for its tasks. Magic Bean acquisition scores every day; UR airships and Legendary Treasure Hunt quests score Tuesday and Saturday. These personal UR tasks are not the guild Airship departure.

      Personal chests, rank & spending wisely
      • Green chest: 660,000 daily points.
      • Blue chest: 1,000,000 daily points.
      • Purple chest: 2,300,000 daily points.

      These are the supplied guide’s thresholds; check the current event track before spending. You do not need to max every day. Even a smaller contribution helps the guild.

      Personal points unlock chests and rank you against guildmates; rank rewards pay daily. In Guild > Guild Duel, look for personal rank and chests on the left and guild-vs-guild score at the top.

      Only matching tasks score: eggs are a Thursday resource, not a Monday task. Palmon XP scores Monday and Thursday. Flexible tasks can fill a shortfall, but do not buy resources just to chase a chest.

      Saturday safety & Raid travel

      Shield up or be online. If absent, arrange protection before the Raid starts. Check shield duration and which actions break it; do not assume an attack or reinforcement leaves protection intact.

      • Do not leave troops idle in an unshielded camp. The supplied guide suggests a shielded camp or gathering far away; distance is not guaranteed protection.
      • Check hospital capacity before fighting; it does not guarantee that all losses are healable.
      • Report enemy marches immediately. Reinforce guildmates only in coordination with officers and after checking protection rules.

      While a Guild Raid is active, members can travel to the opposing guild’s location to fight, and enemies can visit ours. This cross-server Camp relocation does not require a Camp Mover (Targeted). This applies to Raid travel, not every teleport. Confirm the active option and current costs before moving; troops and resources can be lost.

      First match? Capturing & travel

      Prep · 3 minutes: enter during minutes 0–3 to bring your deployment points into the match as Guild and Personal Points. Place guild pins and find your caller; no dispatching yet. Late entrants receive no deployment points.

      • 3 minutes: Solar Vaults open. 8 minutes: War Altars. 11 minutes: Healing Oases. 15 minutes: the central Miracle Temple.
      • Send a squad to a structure: empty means capture; defended means a fight.
      • A garrison is a squad stationed inside to defend and score. Garrisoning is playing, even without a fight.
      • Only one PvP-ready squad? That is enough. Weak extra squads can get wiped quickly.
      • Squads return to camp before redeploying; travel takes minutes. Sandstorm-only march speedups help.
      • Neutral buildings have a 1-minute resistance phase before full occupation. Already occupied buildings have no resistance phase. Full occupation triggers a 1-minute ceasefire, preventing attacks.
      • Protect camps carrying deployment points: defeating one awards those points to the attacker.
      • Squeakbandits spawn in the four corners every 2 minutes after an unspecified initial delay. Defend buildings: bandit occupation turns them neutral, requiring recapture.
      Personal points & rewards

      Earn personal points by attacking structures, garrisoning (occupation points), and attacking enemy camps. Ask your caller for a scoring assignment instead of leaving your post.

      The battle overview shows team score and allied ranking. A number above a camp is the points it carries; beat it to take them. Your camp can be hit too.

      • Participants: Prestige, Golden Leaves, Evolution Energy choice chests and speedups.
      • Winning players: extra Prestige, Dreamium V, speedups and resources.
      • Winning guild, all members: extra Palmon eggs, Golden Leaves, Dreamium V, speedups and resources.

      Current reward tiers and eligibility are shown in the event tab.

      Squad wiped? Healing & getting back in

      Armigo do not die in Sandstorm. They go to the event hospital, which has unlimited capacity. Injured units recover automatically when you leave the battlefield. Medical Aid allows up to 500 assists per match.

      Check the healing item type before confirming, then ask your caller where to go. You earn event speedups by playing.

      If you are unsure what a call means, ask in guild chat, Discord or voice before committing to a march. Report enemy marches and unusually strong players when you see them.

      SANDSTORM SCUFFLE · OFFICER PLAYBOOK

      For officers & callers

      Roster planning, structure priorities and scoring reference. Players can follow their assigned caller without reading these notes.

      Latest PHNX results ↗ · Player guide ↗

      Roster, roles & opening plan

      Fill the roster, appoint one caller per squad and arrange backups for absences.

      1. Lanes · mid accounts: take our two Solar Vaults as prep ends, then support.
      2. Strike · top accounts: our War Altar first, then the Miracle Temple.
      3. Garrison · lower accounts: backfill structures Strike leaves. A Vault pays the same regardless of who holds it.

      Backfill before moving: confirm replacement coverage. Then hold, contest the Temple and punish empty or thin enemy structures. Camps carrying points are secondary targets after objectives are covered.

      If an enemy whale can solo a building, track their squads and take the rest of the map instead of feeding that fight.

      Structures, buffs & confirmed unlocks

      Current 40-minute rules. Unlock times are minutes elapsed from battle start. First occupation and ongoing income below are Guild / Personal Points.

      Solar Vault · 3 minutes

      4 per map. First occupation: 5,000 / 5,000. Ongoing: 10 / 2 per second.

      War Altar · 8 minutes

      2 per map. First occupation: 8,000 / 8,000. Ongoing: 5 / 1 per second. Guild bonus: +10% Palmon ATK, DEF and HP.

      Healing Oasis · 11 minutes

      2 per map. First occupation: 8,000 / 8,000. Ongoing: 5 / 1 per second. Guild bonus: +25% Armigo healing speed.

      Miracle Temple · 15 minutes

      1 at the map center. First occupation: 10,000 / 10,000. Ongoing: 50 / 10 per second.

      Suggested priority: Miracle Temple → War Altar → our Solar Vaults → Healing Oasis. Keep our Altar for its combat buff; take an Oasis when free or healing is needed, not at the expense of the Temple.

      Scoring & protecting points

      First occupation and continued holding earn Guild and Personal Points. The higher Guild Points total wins after 40 minutes.

      Continuous Guild Points are protected during ceasefire. Outside ceasefire, 40% are unprotected and stored on the building. When it is captured, those unprotected points drop around it. Squads can collect them; all collected points are protected. Assign collection as well as defense.

      Matchmaking considers total historical participant power and past squad performance. Squads A/B match independently by their own power but share a pool: A can face A or B. Current power alone does not determine the opponent.

      Confirm attendance before finalizing Squad A, which cannot be cancelled. Squad B can be cancelled if activity does not support a second roster.
